Diwali, Thanksgiving and winter holidays

Flying for the holidays? Let's find them company.

Whether Maa and Papa are coming to you for Thanksgiving or heading home for Diwali, tell us about their trip. We'll look for another family whose parents are on the same flight, or landing at the same time.

How this pilot works

  • A real person on our team reads every trip and looks for matches by hand while the app is in testing.
  • If we find a family on the same flight, or landing within about 90 minutes, we email you both.
  • Nothing about you is shared until both families say yes.
  • It's free. A companion is company, not a carer, so book the airline's wheelchair help too if it's needed.
