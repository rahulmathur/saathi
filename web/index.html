The problem

A 20-hour journey, a connection in a foreign airport, and you on the other side of the world.

Every year, thousands of parents fly alone to visit their children abroad. Families worry about the layover, the immigration form, the wheelchair that doesn't turn up, and not knowing where Maa is for most of a day.

Today, the answer is a post in a Facebook group and hoping a stranger replies. We think families deserve better.

How it works

Four steps, and your parent is never on their own.

1

Add the flight

Paste the airline's booking email. Saathi reads the flight, date and seat for you.

2

Meet a companion

See who else is on that flight or landing nearby. Filter by language, fellow seniors or young helpers.

3

Families agree first

You request an introduction. Contact details are shared only when both families say yes.

4

Follow the journey

Your parent taps through each step. You see every update, and get an alert if they need help.

For you

Find company you can trust.

  • Matches on the same flight, or arriving or leaving within 90 minutes
  • Filter by a shared language like Hindi, Gujarati or Tamil, not just English
  • Choose a fellow senior for company, or a young traveller happy to help with bags and forms
  • Only first names are shown until both families agree
For your parent

Big text, their language, one step at a time.

  • Opens with a 6-digit code from you. No email, no password
  • Switch between English and Hindi at any time, with more languages planned
  • Reads itself aloud, with large buttons to call you or their companion
  • A step-by-step guide from check-in to baggage claim, and a big red "I need help" button
On travel day

Know where they are, without calling every hour.

  • Updates as they check in, board, land and clear immigration
  • An instant alert, with a call button, if they press "I need help"
  • Their companion's family is told too, so help is right there

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is Saathi different from other companion sites and groups?

Most options help you find someone on the same flight and then leave the rest to you. Saathi goes further:

  • It stays with them all day. Your parent gets a step-by-step guide on their phone, and you get an update at every step.
  • It's built for the parent, not just for you. Hindi and English, large text, read-aloud and a 6-digit code instead of a password.
  • A help button that reaches everyone. One tap alerts you and their companion's family.
  • Smarter matching. Same flight, or landing within 90 minutes, filtered by home language.
  • Private by default. First names only until both families agree to an introduction.
  • Adding a trip takes seconds. Paste the airline's booking email and the details fill in.
How much does it cost?

Saathi is free while we test it. If we add paid features later, we'll ask in the survey first and tell you before anything changes.

Who will my parent be travelling with?

Another traveller whose family has registered their flight: usually a fellow senior, sometimes a younger traveller who has offered to help. You see their first name, age, home city, languages and anything they've shared before you request an introduction.

How do you check people?

Every account is confirmed by email, and families talk to each other before the trip. Contact details are only shared once both families agree.

Before launch we plan to add ID checks and vouching by community associations. The survey asks what would make you trust a match, and your answers will shape this.

Is a Saathi companion a carer?

No. A companion is company: someone to sit with, walk with and keep an eye out. If your parent needs a wheelchair or medical help, book the airline's assistance as well. On flights to and from the US, airlines must provide wheelchair assistance free of charge. Our blog explains how to request it.

Does my parent need a smartphone?

A smartphone gives them the journey guide and help button. Without one, you can still find them a companion and follow the trip from your phone. Their companion can also message you.

Which routes and languages?

We're starting with flights between India and the US, in English and Hindi. Matching works on any international flight, and the survey asks which languages and routes to add next.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to run Saathi. We never sell it and never show phone numbers publicly. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time. See our privacy policy.
